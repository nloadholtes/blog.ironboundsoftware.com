# Planning

I've never been terribly good at planning things out. As a kid I was always surprised at how assignments were suddenly "due tomorrow". As an adult, I feel like I never get ahead of the curve. There's always something popping up.

I thought AI would help me with this, and to be fair it has a little bit. But more than anything it seems like things needing my direct input come at me more often and faster than ever.

I have used the pomodoro method in the past to get focused on the execution of my plans, and that has helped get things done faster. But everytime I present a plan or give a status update (work, home, where ever) someone without fail will ask about something I've never considered. (And then they tend to fixate on it and ask a million more questions.)

Clearly I should get better at this.

Fun fact: I tried to plan out the sale of a house, moving, and my wife giving birth to our son. Not only did it wind up not happening in that order, it all happened in a 2 week span. So I'm not allowed to plan stuff at home anymore.
