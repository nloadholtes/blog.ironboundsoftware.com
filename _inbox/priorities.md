# Priorities

A little while back I heard something to the effect of "Never say you didn't have time, say it wasn't a priority."

That really has stuck with me because it reframes a common complaint everyone has. Instead of us being a "victim" of something, we should be up front and just say that there were other things we thought were more important.

THEN comes the hard questions: are you happy with what you felt was more important?

And you you can't answer yes to that question, then you have to look in the mirror and ask yourself some really hard questions.

Pick your priorities. And then show the world you mean it.

And if time runs out? You are probably spending it on things that aren't priorities.
