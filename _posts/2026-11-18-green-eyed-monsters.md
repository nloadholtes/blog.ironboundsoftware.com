---
title: "Green eyed monsters"
date: 2026-11-18
---

With the catch of the starship this weekend the predictable happened yet again: tons of criticism of what is an obvious engineering success. 

This Greek chorus of losers always pops up whenever someone does something impressive in the real world. People who have never done anything of note love to point out how because of their opinions the accomplishment is obviously bad...

Teddy Roosevelt famously pointed out these people in his "Man in the arena" speech over one hundred years ago. Elon and others are trying mighty things. These critics? They do nothing but point and bitch. 

I personally am thrilled to be alive and see these amazing fears feats being accomplished. Do I wish I was doing it? Hell yeah! Am I going to be critical of people because of that? Hell no! 

Go out and build. Strive. Archive. 

Leave those critics where they belong, in the shadows to be forgotten when the sun comes out.
