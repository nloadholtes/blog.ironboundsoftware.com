I am conflicted. The "I stand with Jensen" petitions seem like an idea I agree with. I think this rapid removal from Nvida was... premature. But I can't bring myself to sign on with that particular petition.

I think that what he has confessed to (infidelity) is a problem. Putting aside the accusations of the honeypot spy aspect, this sounds like behavior that should not be rewarded or encouraged. It is not what I would want to see in anyone I am doing business with.

But at the same time the board of Nvidia should take a beat and not just fire/accept his resignation just yet.

I am not trying to say "profit first", but at the same time why are they moving so fast on this? Are the facts settled and known? As we are on the outsided of this drama many of use watching might never know.

Comparing Jenson to Steve Jobs' ouster from Apple is also imprefect: Being "difficult" vs being unfaithful are not the same thing.

I guess it boils down to what we value more. Or how little/much we have thought about what we value when encounter a situation like this.
